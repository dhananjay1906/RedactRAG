# Equipment and IT Security Policy

Harbor County Probation Department, Policy IT-410

## Purpose

This policy sets rules for the use of Department equipment and information systems. It protects the confidential information of clients, victims and employees, and it explains what each employee must do to keep that information safe. It applies to employees, contractors and volunteers.

## Equipment Issued to Staff

Each employee receives the equipment their job requires. Officers receive a laptop, a Department phone and a radio. Field officers also receive a protective vest, a body-worn camera and a pepper spray canister. Equipment remains the property of the Department and must be used for Department work only.

Employees sign for each item on the equipment form when they receive it. Equipment that is lost or stolen must be reported to the supervisor within 1 hour of the employee learning it is missing, and to the IT Service Desk at the same time. If the loss is due to carelessness, the employee may be charged the replacement cost, which is $850 for a laptop, $400 for a phone, and $1,100 for a body-worn camera. When employment ends, all equipment must be returned within 3 working days.

## Vehicles

Department vehicles are checked out from the motor pool using the reservation system. A vehicle may be kept for no more than 12 hours per checkout unless a supervisor approves a longer period. Drivers must hold a valid license, complete the defensive driving course every 3 years, and report any accident or damage within 1 hour. Vehicles may not carry passengers other than clients being transported on official business, and every transport of a client must be logged.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and may not repeat any of the last 12 passwords. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for the case management system. Employees must never share a password, write it where others can see it, or store it in a browser. The Department provides an approved password manager.

After 5 failed login attempts, an account is locked for 30 minutes. The IT Service Desk may unlock it earlier after verifying the identity of the employee. Screens automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, and employees must lock their screen manually whenever they leave a desk.

## Data Classification

The Department classifies information into three levels:

- Public: information approved for release, such as press releases and policy documents.
- Internal: information for staff use only, such as schedules and procedures.
- Restricted: client files, test results, victim information and personnel records.

Restricted information may only be stored in approved systems. It may not be sent by personal email, text message or consumer chat apps. Restricted files sent outside the Department must be sent through the secure transfer portal, and the link expires after 7 days.

## Devices and Removable Media

Department laptops are encrypted with AES-256 and must never be left in a vehicle overnight. The use of USB flash drives and other removable storage is prohibited. Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security Manager, and any approved drive must be encrypted and registered. Personal devices may not be used to access case data. Employees may use personal phones for the multi-factor authentication app only.

## Software and Updates

Only software approved by IT may be installed. Employees may not turn off antivirus software or the firewall. Critical security updates are installed automatically within 72 hours of release, and other updates within 14 days. A laptop that has not been restarted for 7 days will be restarted automatically during the night.

## Email and Phishing

Employees who receive a suspicious email must report it with the Report Phish button and must not click links or open attachments. If an employee clicks a suspicious link or enters their password on an unknown site, they must report it to the IT Service Desk immediately and change their password. Employees complete phishing training every year, and employees who fail 3 simulated phishing tests in a year must repeat the training.

## Incident Response and Breach Notification

Any suspected security incident, such as a lost device, unauthorized access or exposed records, must be reported to the IT Service Desk at once. The IT Security Manager classifies the incident within 4 hours. If a breach of restricted data is confirmed, the Department notifies affected people and the state authority within 72 hours of confirmation. A written incident review is completed within 30 calendar days.

## Backups and Retention

Server data is backed up every night. Backups are kept for 35 days and are encrypted. Email is retained for 3 years. Employees may not delete records subject to a legal hold. Laptops that are retired have their drives wiped using a method that meets government standards, and a certificate of destruction is kept for 5 years.

## Monitoring

The Department monitors use of its systems to protect them and to enforce policy. Employees should not expect privacy when using Department equipment or accounts.
