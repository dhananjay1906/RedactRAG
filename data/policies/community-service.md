# Community Service Policy

Harbor County Probation Department, Policy SUP-240

## Purpose

This policy explains how clients who are ordered by the court to complete community service hours are placed with a work site, how hours are recorded, and what happens when hours are not completed on time. It applies to all adult clients with a community service condition and to the officers who supervise them.

## Placement

Every client with a community service order attends a placement interview within 14 calendar days of the start of supervision. At the interview the Community Service Coordinator reviews the court order, the client's work and school schedule, any physical limits and any transport needs, and then assigns the client to an approved work site. Clients may not arrange their own placement with an organization that is not on the approved site list.

The Department keeps a list of approved work sites, which includes food banks, animal shelters, parks crews, thrift stores run by charities and public libraries. A site is approved for 2 years at a time and must sign a site agreement that names a site supervisor. Clients may not serve at a site where a family member works, at a site where they are currently employed, or at any site that serves children if the court order or the risk assessment prohibits contact with minors.

A client who has a documented medical condition may ask for a desk-based placement. The request must be supported by a note from a licensed provider, and the Coordinator decides the request within 5 working days.

## Hours and Scheduling

Clients must complete at least 8 hours of community service per month until the full order is complete, unless the court sets a different rate. A single shift may not be longer than 10 hours, and hours worked beyond 10 in one day are not credited. Travel time to and from the site is not counted.

The site supervisor signs a timesheet at the end of every shift. The client hands in signed timesheets at the next check-in, and the officer enters the hours in the case management system within 2 working days. Timesheets that are altered, unsigned or turned in more than 30 days after the shift are not accepted.

Clients who are employed full time may ask to complete hours on weekends only. Clients who are enrolled in school may count up to 20 hours of approved school volunteer work toward their order, if the volunteer coordinator at the school signs the timesheet.

## Converting Hours

The court may allow a client to convert unpaid fines to community service hours. The Department uses a conversion rate of $15 of fines for each hour worked. Conversion requires a written court order; officers may not convert fines on their own.

## Missed Shifts and Non-Compliance

A client who cannot attend a scheduled shift must tell the site supervisor at least 24 hours in advance. A shift missed without notice counts as an unexcused absence. After 2 unexcused absences at the same site, the site may ask the Coordinator to remove the client, and the client is reassigned to a new site within 10 working days.

If a client falls more than 40 hours behind the monthly schedule, the officer must hold a compliance meeting within 7 working days. At the meeting the client and officer agree on a catch-up plan in writing. A client who fails to follow the catch-up plan is handled under the graduated response guide. The officer files a report with the court only if the client is still behind 60 days before the supervision end date.

## Safety

Sites must provide any safety equipment needed for the work, such as gloves and high-visibility vests. Clients may not operate power tools, drive site vehicles or handle cash. A client who is injured at a site must report the injury to the site supervisor the same day and to their officer within 24 hours. The Department does not provide workers' compensation for community service, but each approved site must carry liability insurance of at least $500,000.

## Completion

When the client completes the full order, the officer confirms the total in the case management system and sends a completion notice to the court within 5 working days. The client receives a copy of the notice. Hours completed in excess of the order are not carried over to any other case.
